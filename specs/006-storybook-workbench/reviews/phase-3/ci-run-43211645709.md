## T046h: shard size and split count (for the operator's approval; plan.md is not changed)

The shard model, from the measured times:
- A shard's time is a fixed part plus `size` × the per-plant time.
- The fixed part is the job setup, the toolset removal and both pulls: job time minus 8 × 38.1 s. That gives 174 to
  233 s for shards 3 to 6, and 356 s for shard 7.
- The per-plant time is about 1.47 s × the root's story-themes, i.e. 1.47 × (2 × stories + 2) for a plant with one
  story of its own: 38.2 s with the repository's 12 stories.

A shard stays under 600 s while `size` × 1.47 × (2s + 2) ≤ 600 − F, where s is the number of repository stories and F
the fixed part. Two options:

| Option | F (worst measured) | `size` | Shards | Split count s (one shard of `size` passes 10 min above it) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: keep the toolset removal | 360 s | 6 | 9 | 12 stories, the repository's count today: no story can be added |
| A': the same, smaller | 360 s | 4 | 13 | 19 stories |
| B: drop the toolset removal (shards end with 26G free; the removal itself costs 54 to 151 s) | about 210 s (shard 7's 356 s less its 151 s removal) | 8 (unchanged) | 7 | 15 stories |
| B': the same, smaller | about 210 s | 6 | 9 | 21 stories |

Shard 7's 151 s removal is the largest single term after the plants, so dropping the removal (B, B') buys the most per
shard. Whether the disk allows it rests on the run before removal, which this run did not measure. The first
`df -h /` comes after the removal.
