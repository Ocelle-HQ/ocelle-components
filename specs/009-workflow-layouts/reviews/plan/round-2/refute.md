| PLAN-FRESH-2-006 | confirmed | fix | - | contracts/overflow-region.md § Use binds `data-fade=${this.#region.fadeAttribute}`, and § Outputs types `fadeAttribute` as `"" \| "start" \| "end" \| "both"`.
