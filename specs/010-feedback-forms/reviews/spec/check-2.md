| SPEC-2-01 | blocking | spec.md "What 'announced' means here"; FR-008 ("The notice's text"); US1 scenarios 11, 19; FR-027 | FR-008 defines the notice's text on the DOM side (text nodes, `ocelle-icon` `label`, `img` `alt`, skipped subtrees). An announcement, though, is measured as the *region's text in the accessibility-tree snapshot*, and the spec never says how that text is read from the tree. A live region's node has no name from content, and its descendants are a mix of StaticText, link, img and InlineTextBox nodes. | SPEC-2-02 | blocking | spec.md FR-009 table, row "none, or any role other than `alert` and `status`"; "What 'announced' means here" (live region node defined by role `status`, `alert` or `aria-live`); US1 scenario 15 | The row says a notice with any other host role has "no live region node at or inside the host" without `announce`, and exactly one with it.
