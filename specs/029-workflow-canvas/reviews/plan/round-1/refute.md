## Independent checks

Pinned Chromium 156.0.8078.4 reproduced all four geometry and focus findings with native controls in an SVG fixture. The lower action retained focus while its center hit the upper action. A same-parent move preserved the lower action's identity and dropped focus to the document body. The clipped Move button remained a Tab stop with its right edge at −488.125 CSS pixels. The captured pointer stayed at client Y 150 while the SVG top moved from 108 to 68, changing local Y from 42 to 82.

The measured 12-node runs give median viewport and drag update ranges of 0.10–0.20 ms for SVG and 0.20–0.30 ms for Canvas2D. Viewport frame interval p95 ranges were 16.8–17.1 ms and 16.8–16.9 ms respectively. Drag frame interval p95 ranges were 16.8–21.8 ms and 16.7–16.8 ms respectively.
