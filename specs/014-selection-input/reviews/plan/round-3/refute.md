| PLAN-FRESH-3-001 | confirmed | fix | - | I re-ran the lens's `focus.html`, which follows the R-03 algorithm as written. The probe used a real Tab key and real mouse clicks. With no host listener, Tab lands on the outside button. With a host `focusout` listener that moves the focused option in a microtask, focus ends on the option again, for both Tab and a click on the outside button: the batch sees `activeElement` = BODY, `blurred` = a, and a in `removedNodes`. If the listener removes the option instead, focus moves to `b`. If the move runs in `requestAnimationFrame`, the batch runs before the `setTimeout(0)` clear and sees `activeElement` = the button. The "nothing inside the parent" clause then refocuses `a`. So R-03's "task of the blur" scoping and data-model § Selection state / § Transitions ("focused child removed", "child moved") trap keyboard and pointer users who leave the listbox, tabs or segmented choice. That breaks WCAG 2.1.2 and Constitution III. contracts § Pairings has only the later-task negative pair, so no planned test fails. I tested the lens remedy in my own page (`rik3/focus2.html`). It passes every case. All 6 user blurs keep focus where the user put it: Tab and click with a microtask move or removal, a click with a rAF move, and a click on the body with a microtask move. Moves at the end (`appendChild`) and at the same position (`insertBefore(a, b)`) still refocus `a` (US1-21). `a.remove()` moves focus to `b` (FR-011). The round-2 later-task pair keeps focus on the body. User Tab and click blurs carry `relatedTarget` = the target. A removal-induced blur has `relatedTarget` = null, and the microtask queued from its `focusout` finds the records through `takeRecords()` before any host microtask runs. Fix in R-03, data-model § Selection state and § Transitions, and plan phase 2: a `focusout` with a non-null `relatedTarget` records nothing. Otherwise the `focusout` handler queues a microtask that calls `takeRecords()` and treats the child as "had focus" only if those records list it in `removedNodes`, then processes them as the batch. Drop `setTimeout(0)` and the "nothing inside the parent" clause, so only the body or `null` qualifies. Record the measured order in the phase-2 spike. Add the § Pairings row "Tab out while the host re-renders on blur": a host move or removal in a microtask and in rAF, with a real Tab and a real click on an outside button, reading `document.activeElement`. Its positive pair is US1-21. Plants: the record scoped only by its task, and the "nothing inside the parent" clause. |
