Two layout claims were checked in headless Chrome (`/usr/bin/google-chrome`) on a scratch page. In a 300 px grid,
a 120-character slug with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` next to `abc`:

| Track | Slug, hooked | Slug, no hook | Short text |
|-------|--------------|---------------|------------|
| `auto auto` | 277.8 px | – | 150.5 px |
| `minmax(0, 1fr)` twice | – | 150.0 px | – |
| `1fr 1fr` | 150.0 px | 852.2 px | – |

A second page showed that `outline: var(--ocelle-focus-width) solid var(--ocelle-color-focus)` with no tokens loaded
computes `outline-style: none`. With longhands and a literal `solid`, it computes `solid`.

| SPEC-2-01 | blocking | spec.md US4 scenario 1, SC-005, US4 Independent Test | The spec says the hooked identifier's column is "exactly as wide as the same column holding a short text" in an `auto` or `minmax(0, 1fr)` track. An `auto` track grows to max-content when there is free space, so the hooked slug's column is 277.8 px against 150.5 px for a short text (measured above). The test cannot pass.
